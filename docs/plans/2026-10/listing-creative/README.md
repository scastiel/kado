# Listing creative — composition mocks (#131)

Two compositions for the universal App Store creative (5244×2950, shown here at 1600px), compared before one went into `Scripts/frame-screenshots.swift`.

| Mock | |
|---|---|
| `mock-a-device.png` | The headline beside the Today screen in a phone. **Picked.** |
| `mock-b-device-widgets.png` | The same, with the medium and small widget tiles leaning on the phone's edge. |

Why A: the creative is one idea for a first-time visitor, and the tiles in B said the same thing twice — they repeat the list's own rows at a smaller size — while covering the "Slipped" row, the one that makes *without the all-or-nothing* visible. B also pushes the group's weight to the right of the safe band.

A on its own read as empty, so the shipped version, `final.png`, adds a text column above and below the headline: the app icon with "Kadō" in Fraunces, and the `稼 働 · in operation` wordmark from the foot of Settings. Under the wordmark it closes on a slice of the Overview grid in the monochrome sage theme: four habits × two weeks, with one miss, some partial days, and the scheduled habits' off days.

Both crops of A, and of the final, were checked by centring 21:9 (5244×2247) and 3:2 (4425×2950) cuts: headline and phone stay whole in each with margin. Apple's real crops are still to be confirmed in App Store Connect's Preview tool.
