Kadō is a mindful habit tracker that gives you a clear, honest picture of how you're building your routines — without the all-or-nothing guilt of a streak counter.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A HABIT SCORE, NOT JUST A STREAK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Kadō's core algorithm rewards long-term consistency. One missed day doesn't wipe your progress. A strong stretch matters more than a perfect one. You see the trend, not a fragile chain.
